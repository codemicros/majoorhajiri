Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Majdoor Hajiri website. For the mobile application's privacy practices, see the app privacy policy.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

1. About this website

Majdoor Hajiri is a product website published by CodeMicros. The current website is a static website intended to explain the product, show app screenshots and provide links to the Android and iOS apps when they are available.

2. Information collected by this website

The current static website does not include a user account system, database, contact form API or custom tracking code. The hosting provider may process standard technical logs such as IP address, browser information, request time and requested pages for security and delivery purposes.

3. App store links and third-party websites

When the app is published, the website may link to Google Play, Apple App Store and CodeMicros pages. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Analytics and advertising

Analytics or advertising services, including Google AdSense, are not enabled in the initial website package. If they are added later, this privacy policy should be updated before deployment to explain the cookies, identifiers, consent choices and third-party processing involved.

5. Cookies

The initial static website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Browser or hosting-provider behavior may still involve strictly necessary technical storage.

6. Children

This website is intended to describe a business/productivity application for contractors, builders, supervisors and worker-management teams. It is not designed as a service directed to children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the app, analytics, advertising or website functionality changes. The updated date will be shown on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this website, contact codemicros@gmail.com or visit codemicros.com.
