Privacy Policy for Majdoor Hajiri

This policy covers the Majdoor Hajiri mobile application. For the website's own privacy practices, see the website privacy policy.

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Majdoor Hajiri ("we", "our", or "the App") is a worker attendance and wage management application designed for construction sites and other work sites.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Majdoor Hajiri application.

1. Information We Collect

Account and Authentication Information

To allow you to create and access your account, Majdoor Hajiri uses Firebase Authentication.

Depending on the login method you choose, we may receive and process your email address and an authentication identifier associated with your account.

Majdoor Hajiri supports:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Apple Sign-In
  • Email and password authentication

We do not intentionally collect your phone number, address, government ID, or other unnecessary personal information for account creation.

When using Google or Apple Sign-In, authentication is handled through the respective authentication provider. We receive only the information required to authenticate your account and provide the App's services.

2. Site, Worker, Attendance and Wage Data

The App allows you to enter and manage information related to your construction sites and workers.

This may include:

  • Construction/site name
  • Site type
  • Worker name or identifier entered by you
  • Worker designation
  • Daily wage
  • Attendance status
  • Half-day attendance
  • Overtime hours
  • Wage calculations
  • Payment information entered by you
  • Attendance and wage records
  • Other information that you voluntarily enter into the App

This information is used to provide the attendance, worker management, wage calculation, reporting, backup, and export features of the App.

You are responsible for ensuring that any information you enter about workers is collected and used lawfully and with appropriate permission where required.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Allow you to sign in and use the App
  • Store and synchronize your site and attendance data
  • Manage workers and their attendance
  • Calculate wages and overtime
  • Generate attendance and wage records
  • Provide backup and restore functionality
  • Enable data export and sharing features
  • Maintain and improve the App
  • Protect the security and integrity of the App
  • Respond to support requests when applicable

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Majdoor Hajiri uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication and Firebase database services.

These services may process information necessary to provide authentication, data storage, security, and related functionality.

Depending on the Firebase services enabled in the App, technical information such as device information, IP address, app information, diagnostics, or crash information may be processed by Google.

For more information, please review Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Storage and Security

Your account and application data may be stored using Firebase services.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may use trusted service providers, including Firebase/Google services, where necessary to operate and provide the App.

The App may also allow you to voluntarily export or share attendance and wage information through other applications, such as WhatsApp. When you choose to share information using another application, that information is handled according to the privacy policy and practices of the receiving service.

7. Data Backup and Restore

If you use the backup feature, your application data may be stored using the services configured to provide backup functionality.

You can use the available restore functionality to restore your latest available backup.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of important information.

8. Children's Privacy

Majdoor Hajiri is intended for contractors, supervisors, employers, and other users who manage workers and attendance.

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You may stop using the App at any time.

Where supported by the App, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

You may also uninstall the App from your device at any time. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete data already stored on our servers or third-party services, so please contact us if you want to request account or data deletion.

10. Data Retention

We retain account and application information for as long as necessary to provide the App's services or as required for legitimate business, legal, security, or operational purposes.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable requirements and technical limitations.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means that you acknowledge the updated policy.
