Construction site roles, labour attendance and wage records

A construction site can include contractors, engineers, supervisors and many types of skilled or unskilled workers. Majdoor Hajiri keeps the daily attendance and payment record focused on the worker, whatever the role.

Management

Thekedar / Contractor

A contractor or thekedar arranges labour, coordinates work and often needs a clear daily record of who worked, each worker’s rate, advances, payments and pending hisaab.

Project

Builder / Developer

A builder manages project execution and may review labour deployment, subcontractor teams, site progress and worker costs across construction activities.

Engineering

Civil Engineer / Site Engineer

A civil or site engineer supervises technical work, quantities, quality and progress. A reliable labour attendance record helps connect workforce availability with day-to-day site planning.

Supervision

Supervisor / Foreman

The supervisor or foreman coordinates workers on the ground and is often the person who checks daily presence, work type, half day, overtime and team allocation.

Skilled labour

Mason / Raj Mistri

Masons work on brickwork, blockwork, plaster and related masonry activities. Their attendance can be tracked as daily-wage or monthly work depending on the site arrangement.

Skilled labour

Carpenter / Shuttering Carpenter

Carpenters may work on formwork, shuttering, doors, furniture or joinery. Worker-wise attendance makes it easier to review days worked and payment history.

Skilled labour

Fabricator / Welder

Fabricators and welders handle steel and metal work. Their agreed daily rate, attendance, overtime, advance and due amount can be recorded worker by worker.

Site support

Helper / General Labour / Mazdoor

Helpers and general labour support material handling, cleaning, mixing, shifting and other site tasks. A simple hajiri record prevents day-to-day attendance from getting lost in paper registers.

Daily wage worker

Payment is commonly linked to attendance and an agreed daily rate. Half Day or Overtime should follow your site policy and worker agreement.

Basic example: payable attendance units × agreed daily rate

Monthly worker

A monthly worker has an agreed monthly salary. Attendance, absences, paid/unpaid days and deductions depend on the employer’s policy and applicable rules.

Use attendance records as the basis for the final monthly settlement

Advance, payment & due

Workers may take an advance before settlement. Keeping advance and payment entries with the attendance record makes the remaining due easier to review.

Clear records: earnings, advance/payment entries and remaining due

Important: Majdoor Hajiri helps maintain attendance and wage records. Final payroll, overtime rates, deductions, minimum wages and statutory obligations should follow the applicable worker agreement, company policy and local law.

Built for daily labour work

Majdoor Hajiri – labour attendance, daily wage and salary hisaab in one app

Majdoor Hajiri is designed for contractors, builders, civil site engineers, supervisors and small businesses that need a simple way to maintain mazdoor hajiri, labour attendance, helper attendance, daily wages, advances, payments and monthly worker hisaab.

Instead of maintaining scattered paper registers, notebooks or separate calculations, the app keeps worker records together. You can add a mazdoor or helper, mark attendance, review a worker’s monthly calendar, record wage information and keep advance or due amounts easier to understand.

Useful for construction sites and daily-wage teams

The workflow is suitable for civil construction sites, contractors, builders, fabricators, painters, masons, carpenters, helpers and other teams where workers are paid daily, weekly or monthly based on attendance and wage records.
